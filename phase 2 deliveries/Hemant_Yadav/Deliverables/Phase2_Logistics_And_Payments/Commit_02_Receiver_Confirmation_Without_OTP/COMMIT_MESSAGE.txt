feat(logistics): implement receiver delivery confirmation without OTP

Provide an authenticated receiver self-service confirmation endpoint, eliminating delivery delays caused by external SMS OTP gateway failures.

- Added POST /logistics/deliveries/{id}/confirm-receipt endpoint.
- Enforced authenticated receiver authorization checks (buyer or admin access only).
- Recorded delivered_at timestamp and confirmed_by_id audit field on the delivery record.
- Transitioned order status to DELIVERED and initiated the 24-hour dispute window.
- Preserved legacy OTP utilities in utils/otp.py for backwards compatibility.
- Validated confirmation authorization in tests/test_delivery_confirmation_phase2.py.
